FLEXI Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 20, 2026

FLEXI is a classroom robot and teaching platform developed by a research team at the University of Washington Tacoma, School of Education. This page explains what information FLEXI collects, why, and where it is stored.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Teachers: name, email address and profile picture from the Google or Microsoft account used to sign in To sign teachers in and find the robot assigned to them
Teachers: lesson materials they create (text and images) To show lessons on the robot
Students: answers during a lesson — whether each answer was correct and how many tries it took So the teacher can follow progress during the lesson
Robot screens: a random, anonymous ID for each connected phone or tablet To connect a screen to the right robot for the day
Usage: the time each session starts To understand how FLEXI is used and fix problems

What we do not collect

  • Student names, student accounts or any other student identifiers.
  • Audio or video. FLEXI reads text aloud on the device but does not record anything.
  • Teachers' email messages, files or calendars. Microsoft sign-in only asks for basic profile information.

Where it is stored

  • Google Firebase (United States): accounts, lessons, student answers and robot settings.
  • Cloudinary: images teachers upload for lessons.

Robot screens also remember which robot and screen they are on in the device's own browser storage.

Who can see it

  • A teacher can see only their own robot's lessons and results.
  • The FLEXI research team can see all data to run and improve the platform.
  • We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until the end of the research study and then deleted. Teachers can ask us to delete their data at any time.
